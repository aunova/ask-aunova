## 3. Basement substructure (MS-03)

Three basement levels, B1 to B3, 24,100 m² of slab. The six-day overrun is attributed to a
re-sequencing of the B2 ramp pour after a late change to the car-stacker layout. The time was
recovered within the podium package; no extension of time was granted and none was claimed.

## 4. Podium and transfer level (MS-04)

Transfer slab at level 4, 1.8 m deep, post-tensioned, poured in four sections between
2026-04-08 and 2026-04-24. Stressing records complete for all 212 tendons. Sixteen tendons
re-stressed following a first-pass elongation shortfall; all subsequently within tolerance.

## 5. Superstructure frame (MS-05, MS-06)

- Concrete cube results: 1,164 samples, 1,164 passes at 28 days. No failures.
- Survey verticality at level 34: maximum deviation 21 mm against a 48 mm permitted envelope.
- Core wall jump-form struck and demobilised 2026-08-27.
