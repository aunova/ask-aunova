## 1. Scope of this record

This record closes the enabling works and substructure package: site clearance, secant
pile shoring to the perimeter, dewatering, bored piling, pile cap construction and the
reinforced raft to the tower footprint.

## 2. Enabling works and shoring (MS-01)

- Secant pile wall, 412 piles, 600 mm diameter, toe level −19.4 m.
- Dewatering wells commissioned 2025-07-02; drawdown held at −18.0 m through the pour.
- Shoring completion inspected on site 2025-09-19 and 2025-09-22.
- **Completed 2025-09-22**, eight days ahead of the 2025-09-30 programme date.

## 3. Piling and raft foundation (MS-02)

- 268 bored piles, 1200 mm diameter, average toe level −34.7 m.
- Pile integrity testing: 268 of 268 by low-strain method, 9 by cross-hole sonic logging.
  All results within the acceptance band agreed in the contract specification.
- Static load test on two sacrificial piles to 1.5 × working load; settlement 7.1 mm and
  6.4 mm respectively, both inside the 12 mm acceptance limit.
- Raft pour: 6 continuous pours, 11,840 m³ total, concrete grade C50/60.
  Peak core temperature 68.2 °C against a 70 °C limit; differential held under 20 K.
- **Completed 2025-12-11**, four days ahead of the 2025-12-15 programme date.

## 4. Outstanding items at the date of this record

1. Two pile caps at grid F/7 and F/8 carry a non-structural honeycombing repair, remedied
   and re-inspected on 2025-12-09. Closed.
2. Cathodic protection monitoring points to be handed to the facilities package at MS-09.

## 5. Statement

On the evidence of the inspections, tests and records listed above, the enabling works and
