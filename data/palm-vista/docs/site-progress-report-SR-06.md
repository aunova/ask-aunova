## 3. Why the package is behind

1. Shipment 7 of 14 (218 panels) was held for eleven days at the consolidation yard after a
   glass-unit edge-deletion defect was found on inspection. 34 panels were rejected and
   re-manufactured; the balance was released on 2026-09-18.
2. Two of the four building maintenance unit cradles were out of service for six working
   days in September for rope replacement.

## 4. Recovery measures agreed with the main contractor

- A second installation crew from 2026-10-13, taking the planned rate from 46 to 68 panels
  per week.
- Re-manufactured panels for shipment 7 confirmed for delivery 2026-10-27.
- At the planned recovered rate the 60% trigger is reached in the week of 2026-11-24, six
  days before the milestone date.
