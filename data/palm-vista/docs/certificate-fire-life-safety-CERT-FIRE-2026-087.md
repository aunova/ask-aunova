## Scope

Stage 2 covers passive and distribution elements installed up to the date of inspection:

- Stairwell pressurisation shafts and plant bases, cores A and B, levels B3 to 20.
- Dry and wet standpipe risers, levels B3 to 20.
- Compartmentation and fire-stopping at slab and riser penetrations, levels B3 to 20.
- Fire-rated door frames set into blockwork, levels B3 to 12.

Stage 2 does **not** cover detection and alarm, sprinkler heads, smoke-control commissioning,
or anything above level 20. Those are stage 3 and are not yet assessed.
