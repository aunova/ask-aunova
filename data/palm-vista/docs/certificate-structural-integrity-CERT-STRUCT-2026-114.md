## Scope of verification

Reinforced concrete core walls, columns, transfer structure and floor slabs, from basement
level B3 to level 34 inclusive. The verification does **not** cover the facade system, the
mechanical, electrical and plumbing installation, or any fit-out element.

## Basis of verification

1. Independent review of the structural design package, revision F, dated 2026-01-30.
2. Witnessed sampling of 118 of 1,164 concrete cube tests across the superstructure pours.
3. Three site inspections: 2026-04-22 (transfer slab), 2026-06-11 (level 12),
   2026-08-26 (level 34, pre-topping-out).
4. Review of survey verticality sets SV-L04, SV-L12, SV-L24 and SV-L34.
5. Review of post-tensioning stressing records for all 212 transfer-slab tendons.

## Findings

- Concrete strength: all reviewed results met or exceeded the specified C50/60 characteristic
  strength at 28 days. Lowest reviewed result 54.1 MPa.
- Reinforcement: spot checks at 14 locations matched the issued-for-construction drawings.
- Geometry: maximum verticality deviation at level 34 is 21 mm, inside the 48 mm envelope.
- Post-tensioning: 16 tendons required re-stressing on first pass; all were subsequently
  within the specified elongation tolerance, and the records are complete.

## Statement

On the basis set out above, the structural frame of Palm Vista Tower from level B3 to
level 34 is verified as constructed in accordance with the reviewed design package and the
project specification.

This certificate is a verification of the structure as inspected to 2026-08-26. It is not a
permission to occupy, and it does not extend to works executed after that date.
