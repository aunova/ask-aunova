## What "provisional" means here

This is a **design-stage** rating. It is awarded on the submitted design package and the
modelled performance only. It becomes a final rating **only** after as-built verification,
which is scheduled against milestone MS-10 and has not taken place.

A provisional rating must not be described as a final or achieved rating, and must not be
described as a certification of the completed building.
