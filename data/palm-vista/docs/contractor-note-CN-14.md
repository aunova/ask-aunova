## Purpose

This note records the commercial position on three delays. It is deliberately **not** part
of the buyer-facing record: it contains the developer's own view of liability and a
negotiating position, and quoting it to a buyer would be both wrong and commercially
damaging.

## 1. MS-03 basement substructure, 6 days late

Cause: late client-side change to the car-stacker layout, instructed 2026-01-19, which
forced a re-sequence of the B2 ramp pour. **The cause is ours, not the contractor's.**
Calder & Roe did not claim an extension of time and recovered the time in the podium
package. No cost consequence. Position: do not reopen.

## 2. MS-07 facade, currently 12 points behind the trigger

Cause: shipment 7 held eleven days at the consolidation yard after an edge-deletion defect
in 34 glass units, plus six working days of building-maintenance-unit cradle downtime.

Commercial position:
- The glass defect sits with the facade supplier under clause 14.2. A contra-charge of
  1,420,000 fils has been raised and is disputed.
- The cradle downtime is Calder & Roe's; they have not claimed for it.
- The second installation crew from 2026-10-13 is at the contractor's cost, not ours.
  **Do not characterise that crew as a goodwill measure by the developer.**

## 3. MS-08 MEP first fix, forecast 28 days late

Cause: chilled-water riser clash with the revised facade bracket grid, levels 6 to 14. The
coordinated model was re-issued 2026-09-21 at a cost of 19 working days.

**The bracket grid revision was instructed by us on 2026-08-11.** Our exposure here is real.
Counsel's preliminary view is that an extension-of-time claim would likely succeed if made.
No claim has been made to date.

## 4. Float position — the thing to watch

MS-09 fit-out holds 42 days of float. The MEP overrun consumes 28 of them. That leaves
14 days protecting the 2027-08-31 handover, down from 42 at the start of the year.

A second event of this size moves the handover date, and handover triggers the largest
instalment on every unit. Finance should be told before the next buyer communication, not
after.

## 5. What may be said publicly

- The facade and MEP packages are behind programme. **True, and already in SR-06 and SR-07.**
- The handover date of 2027-08-31 is unchanged. **True today.**
- "No delays affect handover." **Do not say this.** It is unsupportable from 2027 onwards
  and the float position above is the reason.
